Copy the prompt, paste it into any AI chat, then paste the lessons you want to be tested on.

```prompt Exam practice
Act as my examiner at the Islamic University of Madinah.

Quiz me on the material I paste below.

How to run the quiz:
1. Ask one question at a time and wait for my answer.
2. Mix question types: definitions, "list the conditions", short explanations, and multiple choice.
3. After each answer, say if it's right, correct anything I missed, and quote the line from the material that has the answer.
4. Keep a running list of topics I got wrong.
5. After 10 questions, give me my score and the 3 topics I should revise first.

Use Arabic for technical terms, with English explanations.

Material:
```

## Getting the most out of it

- Answer from memory without looking at your notes. The point is to find what you don't know yet.
- When it finishes, type "Again, only on the topics I got wrong."
- Check anything surprising against your book or teacher. AI can be confidently wrong.
