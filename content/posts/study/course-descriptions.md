Every course at the university has a **توصيف** (course description): the official list of topics it covers (قائمة الموضوعات), its aims and what you're assessed on. Use it to see exactly what's on your syllabus before you study.

Each college has **8 levels**, two per year.

| College | Course descriptions |
| --- | --- |

More colleges will be added. Have the course descriptions for yours? [Send them in](/ask/?type=tip&topic=study).

## Using it with the mind map maker

Upload your course's توصيف to the same project as your book, then type `توصيف` in the [mind map maker](/study/mind-map-maker/). It reads the list of topics and gives you a report of what the course requires, so your mind map covers what's actually on the syllabus.
