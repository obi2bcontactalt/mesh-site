Most subjects here reward two things: knowing the material well enough to explain it, and remembering texts and definitions word for word. Both come from the same habit: pulling information out of your head regularly, instead of re-reading it.

## The three habits that do most of the work

**Test yourself instead of re-reading.** Close the book and write down everything you remember about a lesson, then open it and check what you missed. The gaps are what you study next. Re-reading feels productive, but recall is what makes it stick.

**Spread it out.** Go back over each lesson a day later, then a few days later, then a week later. Each review is shorter than the last, and you remember far more than if you did it all at once.

**Teach it.** Explain the lesson out loud to a classmate, a family member or an empty room, in English and then in Arabic. Wherever you stumble is where you don't really understand it yet.

## A weekly plan

| Day | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Lecture day | Same evening: write a one-page summary from memory, then fill gaps from your notes |
| Next day | Ten minutes: recall the summary without looking, mark anything you missed |
| Weekend | Review the whole week's lessons and turn them into a mind map |
| Each week | Practise questions on older lessons so nothing goes cold before exams |

## Texts and definitions

For anything you need word for word, such as a matn, a definition or a list of conditions:

- Learn it in small pieces, a line or two at a time, and join the pieces only once each one is solid.
- Say it out loud. Hearing yourself helps, and it shows where you hesitate.
- Learn the meaning alongside the words. A definition you understand is much harder to forget.
- Revise in short sessions after Fajr or before sleeping, when the day is quiet.

> [!TIP]
> Use the exam prompt in this section to turn your notes into practice questions, and the mind map prompt to see a whole subject on one page.

## The week before an exam

Stop taking new notes. Spend the time on practice questions and recall, sleep properly, and ask classmates about anything that's still unclear. Last-minute cramming costs more in tiredness than it gains in memory.
