Tried and tested by a mother and her two soft-play experts, aged two and three. Prices are what she paid and may change.

## Soft play centres

### Kinetic
[Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/sjH7sompZUFuZGPL9)

- **Price:** 35 riyals per child
- **Ages:** 1 to about 13
- Really nice and fairly priced. Two floors, and under-threes do best in the upstairs play area. There's a café.

### Poky, inside Wow 5! (5 Riyal Market)
[Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RFS3ftpoG8AVNn8P9)

- **Price:** 20 riyals per child
- **Ages:** 1 to 10
- Cheaper, with fewer activities and not as clean. Two floors; the top floor works very well for babies and toddlers.

### Peekboo
[Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/HHUiSx6Ln8s4DbUq9)

- **Price:** around 60 riyals per child
- **Ages:** 1 to about 14
- Really nice and very clean, but expensive. Has a big café, and the building around it is full of food places and cafés.

### Snowy Forest
[Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/r6c4VE7FCieX8FT19)

## Mall crèches (under 5s)

You can leave under-fives in these soft-play crèches or stay with them.

- **Al Rashid Mall:** basement floor, near Home Centre. Ball pit, slides, activity tables and role-play areas such as a kitchen. There's a TV area, but it isn't always on. Under 5s only, 50 riyals per child.
- **Al Noor Mall:** similar, with a slide into a ball pit and a climbing frame among other activities. Around 40 riyals per child.
- **Al Noor Mall, another play area:** [Location on Google Maps](https://maps.app.goo.gl/1xB4qFEvuGpt1vjA6)

## Gardens and parks

Gardens such as Reef Al Bustan have bouncy castles and rides like little trains.

> [!TIP]
> Mall play and arcade areas, and some of the big bouncy castles in the gardens, aren't really suitable for children under 4. For little ones, the soft play centres above are a better fit.
