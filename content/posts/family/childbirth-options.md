> [!WARNING]
> These are sisters' own experiences, last updated in April 2025. Hospitals change their rules and prices, so check with the hospital before your due date.

## Free for university wives

**Uhud Hospital** is the only free option for wives of Islamic University students. You need your own iqama.

## Who's allowed in

| Question | Hospitals |
| --- | --- |
| Female-only staff | Al-Zahra |
| Husband allowed during labour | Al-Zahra, Saudi German, Mouwasat, Madina National Hospital, Hayat National |
| Husband allowed during the delivery | Mouwasat, Hayat |
| Husband can stay 24/7 after the birth | Saudi German, Al-Zahra, Mouwasat, Madina National Hospital, Hayat |

Whether your husband can be there during labour or delivery is up to the staff and doctors on duty, and he can be turned away.

**Female birth companion:** Uhud and Al-Zahra hospitals used to allow a female companion throughout the delivery (with a security pass first), but the programme was suspended during Covid. Ask whether it has restarted.

**No choice of doctor:** at Mouwasat, Wilaada, Uhud, Madina National Hospital and Dr. Hamid Sulaiman Al-Ahmadi Hospital, the delivery is usually done by whichever doctor is on duty.
