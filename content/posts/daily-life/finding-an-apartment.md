Use several of these at once. Good places go quickly.

## Websites and apps

- **[Haraj](https://haraj.com.sa)** (حراج): the big Saudi classifieds site, with plenty of rentals
- **Aqar** (عقار): a property app with listings by area. Search for **عقار** in your app store.

## Groups

- Telegram: [Durrat Al-Hijra Real Estate Office](https://t.me/DurratAlHijraRealEstateOffice)
- Telegram: [shoqaqtullabmed](https://t.me/shoqaqtullabmed) (flats for students in Madinah)
- WhatsApp: [apartments group](https://chat.whatsapp.com/GVoe4bmZpI09YcaVf0QiPu)

## Drive around

Drive through the neighbourhoods you'd like to live in and look for "for rent" signs. They usually say **للإيجار** and have a phone number to call.
