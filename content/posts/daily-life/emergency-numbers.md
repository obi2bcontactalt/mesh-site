Save these in your phone today, so you don't have to search for them in an emergency.

| Who | Number |
| --- | --- |
| General emergency | **112** |
| Police | **999** or **911** |
| Ambulance | **997** |
| Fire and Civil Defence | **998** |
| Traffic police | **993** |
| Highway patrol | **996** |
| Domestic violence hotline | **1919** |
| Ministry of Health (medical advice) | **937** |

> [!TIP]
> If you're not sure which to call, call **112**. It works from any mobile.
