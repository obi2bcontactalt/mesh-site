A short list of programmes for brothers and children. It's a good way to stay fit and get to know brothers in the city. Full contact details for everything here are on [hijrahnow.com/madinah](https://hijrahnow.com/madinah).

## Martial arts

Contact details: [hijrahnow.com/madinah#martial-arts](https://hijrahnow.com/madinah#martial-arts)

- **Coach Greg (jiu-jitsu):** black belt, trained by Eddie Redzovic. Classes 3 days a week for children and adults, taught in English. Gym in Al-Difa.
- **Coach Masoud (Muay Thai):** boxing, Muay Thai and other combat sports. Coach Masoud holds the Khan 15 rank in Muay Thai, which very few non-Thais have.
- **Captain Neelain (boxing, MMA):** experienced MMA coach and former bodyguard of Muhammad Ali. Boxing, jiu-jitsu and MMA classes for children and teens at Power Lines Sport Center.
- **Coach Ridwan Mustafa (Muay Thai):** classes 6 days a week, 4 to 7pm, for adults and teens. Gym in Sultana.
- **Wolves Boxing Club:** boxing for men and boys, 6 days a week, in Hayy Shadhah.
- **Badr Team BJJ:** the Madinah Muay Thai & BJJ Academy, in the Prince Abdul Aziz Sports Hall. Classes for children and men.
